Expense Monster

Personal budgeting that stays on your own computer.

Expense Monster is a personal finance app built and used by David Richard for personal budgeting. It connects to the owner's bank and credit-card accounts through Plaid and shows spending by category, budgets, recurring charges, and balances.

What data it uses

Through Plaid's Transactions product, the app reads account names, balances, and transactions (date, description, merchant, amount, category). It does not request account or routing numbers or identity information, and it never sees bank login credentials. Those are handled entirely by Plaid.

How the data is handled

  • Stored locally on the owner's own encrypted device. There are no Expense Monster servers.
  • Never shared or sold. No advertising, analytics, or tracking.
  • Deletable at any time. Removing a connection revokes Plaid access and deletes its data.

Details are in the privacy policy.

Who can use it

Expense Monster is a single-user personal project and is not offered to the public.
