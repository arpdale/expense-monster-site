Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026
Operator: David Richard, david.richard@gmail.com

Expense Monster is a personal budgeting application that its operator runs on their own computer, for their own bank and credit-card accounts. This policy explains what data the application handles and how.

What we collect

When a bank is connected through Plaid, Expense Monster receives, via Plaid's Transactions product:

  • account names, types, the last digits of account numbers (masks), and balances;
  • transactions: date, description, merchant name and logo, amount, category, and pending status.

The user may also add rules, budgets, notes, and category changes, and import statement files.

We do not collect full account or routing numbers, identity information (name, address, SSN), or bank login credentials. Bank logins are handled entirely by Plaid and never reach Expense Monster.

How the data is used

Only to show the user their own spending, budgets, recurring charges, and balances. There is no advertising, profiling, analytics, or tracking.

Where the data is stored

On the user's own computer, in a local database, on an encrypted disk. Expense Monster has no servers and does not send financial data anywhere, except for its HTTPS requests to Plaid to retrieve that data.

Sharing

Data is not sold, rented, or shared with anyone. The only third party involved is Plaid, which connects to financial institutions. Plaid's handling of data is described in the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.

Consent

The user chooses which institutions to connect and consents to Plaid's terms in Plaid Link each time one is connected.

Retention and deletion

  • Transactions and balances are kept while a connection is active, up to a rolling maximum of 7 years.
  • Removing a connection revokes its Plaid access token (via Plaid /item/remove) and deletes that connection's data.
  • All data can be deleted at any time by removing the app's local data folder.
  • Plaid connections can also be reviewed and revoked at my.plaid.com.

Security

Data is stored only on a FileVault-encrypted device, readable only by the owner's user account. The app's local server accepts connections only from the device itself, and all communication with Plaid uses TLS.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
